### Guideline

## Assessment Guideline

 # Content Structure Analysis

	Evaluate the document's organizational pattern:

		Structured Indicators:

			1. Hierarchical relationships (parent-child, categories)

			2. Explicit entity connections and dependencies

			3. Taxonomies, classifications, or formal schemas

			4. Cross-references and bidirectional links

			5. Network-like information architecture
 
		Unstructured Indicators:

			1. Narrative text, prose, conversational content

			2. Sequential information flow without explicit relationships

			3. Descriptive passages and explanations

			4. Opinion-based or subjective content
 
# Entity Relationship Assessment

	Count and evaluate entity interconnections:

		High Relationship Density:

			1. Multiple entities with explicit connections

			2. Complex interdependencies between concepts

			3. Formal relationship definitions (is-a, part-of, related-to)

			4. Network effects requiring relationship context
 
		Low Relationship Density:

			1. Standalone information chunks

			2. Minimal cross-references

			3. Self-contained passages

			4. Linear information presentation
 
# Query Pattern Prediction

	Anticipate likely user query types:

		Graph-Optimized Queries:

			1. "How are X and Y related?"

			2. "What connects A to B?"

			3. Multi-hop reasoning questions

			4. Exploratory relationship discovery
 
		Vector-Optimized Queries:

			1. "Find content similar to..."

			2. Semantic search for concepts

			3. "What does the document say about..."

			4. Contextual understanding questions
 
## Classification Categories

# VECTOR_STORE_ONLY

	1. Characteristics: Unstructured content with rich semantics, minimal explicit relationships

	2. Document Types:

			Research papers (methodology, results, discussions)

			News articles and journalistic content

			Product descriptions and marketing materials

			User manuals and procedural guides

			Meeting notes and transcripts

			Blog posts and editorial content

			Literary works and creative content
 
# KNOWLEDGE_GRAPH_ONLY

	1. Characteristics: Highly structured with explicit entity relationships, formal connections

	2. Document Types:

			Organizational charts and hierarchies

			Technical specifications with component relationships

			Academic curricula with prerequisite mappings

			Regulatory frameworks with compliance structures

			Scientific taxonomies and classifications

			Database schemas and data models

			Legal documents with citation networks

			Supply chain documentation
 
# DUAL_INGESTION

	1. Characteristics: Hybrid content with both rich semantics AND explicit relationships

	2. Document Types:

			Medical literature with drug interactions and treatment protocols

			Legal case law with citations and procedural narratives

			Technical documentation with explanatory content and component relationships

			Academic textbooks with concept relationships and detailed explanations

			Business process documents with workflow relationships and descriptive content

			Scientific journals combining data relationships and interpretive content
